🧪 Harmonization Methodology

1 · Sensor continuity gap. Terra/Aqua MODIS (1 km) is approaching end-of-life, while Suomi-NPP/NOAA-20 VIIRS (375 m) detects smaller fires at higher density. Raw counts simply can't be compared head-to-head.

2 · Spatio-temporal clustering. Our Python backend ingests NASA FIRMS CSV streams and runs 1 km / 24 h DBSCAN to merge overlapping detections into unified fire events.

3 · Historical baseline. Monthly totals are benchmarked against the 2003–2025 median so anomalous burning periods pop out immediately.
