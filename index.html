🧪 Harmonization Methodology

1 · Sensor continuity gap. Terra/Aqua MODIS (1 km) is approaching orbital retirement, while Suomi-NPP and NOAA-20/21 VIIRS (375 m) detect small fires with high spatial granularity. Raw counts produce an artificial 3x-5x resolution inflation.

2 · Spatio-temporal clustering. Our Python backend ingests NASA FIRMS streams and runs DBSCAN with a Haversine metric (eps = 1.0 km) and a temporal grouping window ≤ 24h. Overlapping pixel detections are fused into single physical fire events with weighted centroids.

3 · Cross-Sensor FRP Calibration. Fire Radiative Power (FRP) is cross-calibrated between MODIS 4μm and VIIRS 3.75μm I-bands, boosting confidence to High when verified across multiple orbital passes.

4 · Historical climate baseline. Monthly cluster totals are evaluated against the 2003–2025 monthly medians to compute true climate anomaly percentages.
